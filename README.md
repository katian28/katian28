### [Welo](https://www.welofinance.com/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/welo-finance/id6759347599)
**"The finance app you'll actually stick with," live on the App Store**

A social, habit-driven financial literacy app for Gen Z, built with Google x Minerva University. It borrows engagement mechanics from apps like Duolingo and Strava (streaks, social accountability) instead of guilt-based budgeting, to address the ~95% abandonment rate of typical finance apps. Integrated backend API endpoints and Firebase analytics, and optimized the UI with Flutter state management, alongside Google senior engineers.

### [Credit Risk Assessment](https://github.com/katian28/Credit_Risk_Model): AI4ALL Team Project
**Fair lending model for underserved populations, built with a 5-engineer team**

Trained Random Forest and XGBoost models on 36K+ records with 20+ engineered socioeconomic features, reaching a 0.78 F1-weighted score via 5-fold cross-validation. Applied SMOTEENN oversampling and fairness-aware techniques to reduce disparate impact for thin-file borrowers. Selected for the national AI4ALL Student Symposium.

### LLM-as-Judge Email Editing Evaluation
**Built at ServiceNow's AI.Accelerate Program (Dec 2025 to Jan 2026)** · [Certificate](https://github.com/katian28/katian28/blob/main/assets/servicenow-ai-accelerate-certificate.pdf)

Designed an evaluation framework comparing GPT-4.1 and GPT-4o-mini on email-editing tasks (shorten, elaborate, tone change) across faithfulness, completeness, and conciseness. Iterated on prompts to fix a hallucination pattern specific to the "elaborate" action, and documented that GPT-4.1's own judging was biased toward its own outputs over GPT-4o-mini's equivalent responses.
